# Technique sources

Reference text behind Trace's findings, advice and drills, checked 29 September 2026.
Files 1 to 10 are Neil Gresham's UKClimbing technique series (with Nina Tappin,
Steve Golley, Gill Peet); 11 is Gripped's comprehensive guide; 12 is 99boulders on
holds and moves; 13 is REI's climbing techniques page. Plain text captured from the
live pages so the claims Trace makes can be traced to a sentence.

Where Trace and the sources meet:

- Bent arms while static: Gresham, Posture, tip 1 and 2. Default hang is arms
  virtually straight with a slight bend and shoulders engaged; do not sag onto
  straight arms; do not hold lock-offs for more than a few seconds.
- Weight hanging off your arms: Gresham, General approach ("arms straight and hips
  twisted in"); REI (one hip against the wall, weight over the feet); Gripped (hips
  open, groin toward the wall except in a drop-knee).
- Imprecise feet: Gresham, Improve Your Movement, drill 1: silent, no scuff, first
  time, no test, eye on the foot until it is placed.
- Reading the route while hanging: Gresham, General approach, pointer 4: plan from the
  floor, mime the hand sequence.
- Start-stop movement: Gresham, Improve Your Movement, drill 3: flow through fixed
  positions rather than stopping on them.
- Hanging outside your contacts: REI balance tactics; Gripped on flagging without
  pressing into the wall.
- Catching outside the deadpoint: 99boulders deadpoint; Gripped on momentum from the
  hips and the zero-momentum move.

What the sources cover that Trace cannot measure with a 15-joint pose: grip and hold
types (Handholds 1 to 3, Undercuts and sidepulls), heel and toe hooks, which part of
the shoe is on the hold, pockets.

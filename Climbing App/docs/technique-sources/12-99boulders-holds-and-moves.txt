99boulders: climbing moves, holds and technique (extracted)
Holds: crimp (top pad only), jug (whole hand), sloper (open hand, no positive gradient), sidepull (positive part faces sideways, pull laterally), gaston (positive part faces you, push out, shoulder power), undercling (faces down, easier as you move above it), pinch (thumb squeezes), pocket (mono = one finger), guppy (wrap sideways, grip across the palm), edge foothold, smear foothold (friction).
Moves: flag (foot pressed to blank wall for balance and hip locking), rock over (shift weight across onto a foothold before moving up), drop knee (twist knee down and in toward centre to raise the opposite arm), cross through, match, layback (lean away from the hold to find balance on sidepulls without opposition), heel hook (third arm, toes angled down), toe hook (top of shoe, flexed ankle), bicycle, bridge, mantle (from under a hold to above it, heel assist), dyno (sink then explode), deadpoint (snap fingers at apex for a slot or pocket).
